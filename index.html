04 · HISTORY

Five chapters.
One enduring place.

Use the timeline to move from early Egypt to the modern archaeological and tourism destination of today.

The pyramid era · Fourth Dynasty
01

Early Egypt 3100–2600 BCE

Giza became part of the Memphis area and an important burial ground as early rulers developed new methods for large tombs and monuments.

02

Construction of the Pyramids 2600–2500 BCE

The Fourth Dynasty saw the pyramids of Khufu, Khafre and Menkaure, alongside the creation of the Great Sphinx.

03

Later Ancient Period 2500 BCE–600s CE

Giza remained a cemetery and religious site through later Egyptian, Greek and Roman rule.

04

Medieval Period 600s–1800s CE

After the Arab conquest, Cairo and nearby settlements grew while the pyramids remained celebrated landmarks for travelers.

05

Modern Giza 1800s–Today

Giza became increasingly connected to modern Cairo; archaeological study and preservation expanded, and the pyramid area joined the UNESCO World Heritage Site in 1979.
